# TopLet 0.1.15 — Calendar sources and clearer glass

## English

- Choose which iCloud, school, or subscribed calendars appear under Settings → Calendar → Calendar Sources. The agenda includes class times and locations when available. Calendar access works independently of optional Reminders access.
- The expanded notch uses native Clear Glass on macOS 26 and later, with a behind-window blur on earlier versions. Light Glass and Dark Glass replace opaque White and Black. Toolbar and content share one surface; accessibility settings still use a solid fallback.

## 简体中文

- 「设置 → 日历 → 日历来源」可勾选 Mac 上已同步的 iCloud、学校及订阅日历，只显示课表。展开日历可查看课程时间和地点。无需开启提醒事项权限即可加载日历。
- 刘海展开后使用更清透的原生玻璃，旧系统使用窗口背后的原生模糊。「浅色玻璃／深色玻璃」取代实色白／黑，顶栏与内容共用一层材质。系统降低透明度或增强对比度时仍保留实色回退。
