# TopLet 0.1.9 — AirDrop and Clipboard

## English

- Drag files to the top to reveal two targets: AirDrop on the left, Copy to Clipboard on the right. The hovered card highlights immediately; the release position chooses the action.
- Copy several files together, then paste with Command-V in an app that accepts file paste. Inline dragged images, links and text retain their native representations. A short “Copied to Clipboard” confirmation appears after success.
- Copying managed screenshots keeps them on the shelf instead of triggering remove-after-drag. Invalid batches preserve the previous clipboard; locked sessions refuse new drops. Gaps and areas outside the cards do not accidentally start AirDrop.
- English and Simplified Chinese labels and settings are updated; the existing top-drag preference is preserved.

Validation: isolated native drag/clipboard checks, screenshot retention and privacy checks, English/Chinese light/dark card renders, localization audit, full Apple Silicon build, and signed incremental update verification. No real AirDrop transfer is started by tests.

## 简体中文

- 将文件拖到顶部时显示两个目标：左侧 AirDrop，右侧复制到剪贴板。进入区域立即高亮，按松手位置执行对应操作。
- 支持一次复制多个文件，在接受文件粘贴的应用中按 Command-V 即可粘贴。直接拖入的图片数据、链接和文字保留原生格式；复制成功后显示简短提示。
- 复制截图会保留文件架中的截图，不触发“拖出后删除”。无效批次保留原剪贴板，锁屏时拒绝投放；在间隙或卡片外松手不会误触 AirDrop。
- 同步更新中英文文案与设置说明，保留原有顶部拖入开关。

已检查原生拖放与独立测试剪贴板、截图保留、锁屏、中英文深浅色渲染、本地化、完整构建和签名增量更新。测试不会实际发送文件到其他设备。
