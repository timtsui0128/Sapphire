# Sapphire Personal 0.1.4 — Keep song artwork in sync

## English

- Fix the previous song's cover being carried into a new track when the player sends its title before its artwork, as reported with NetEase Cloud Music. Show the music placeholder while the new cover is pending, then display it as soon as its artwork event arrives.
- Reset the artwork grace timer on every track change, including rapid skips. Explicitly supplied shared album covers continue to work.
- Bind remote cover downloads to a specific song and request. Late results cannot overwrite the current cover, including after returning to the same song or switching players. Keep the song identity stable during image loading.
- Includes 0.1.3's screenshot priority: the next ordinary notch click opens newly collected screenshots directly.

Validated with the production media parser/controller and image-loading methods: separate title/artwork events, delayed covers, rapid skips, missing covers, shared album images, player changes, and deliberately out-of-order downloads. Complete Apple Silicon build and code signature verification. These automated checks do not control the user's running player or Sapphire app; player-side delivery delays can still briefly show a placeholder.

## 简体中文

- 修复网易云音乐反馈的封面慢一首问题：播放器先发来新歌名、稍后才发封面时，不再把上一首图片配给新歌。等待期间显示音乐占位图，新封面到达后立即更新。
- 每次切歌重新计算封面等待时间，连续快速切歌也不会沿用旧计时器；同专辑歌曲明确提供相同封面时仍可复用。
- 图片下载绑定具体歌曲和请求，旧请求即使较晚完成、或再次切回同一首歌，也不能覆盖当前封面；加载封面不再修改歌曲标识。
- 包含 0.1.3 的截图优先功能：收到新截图后，下次普通点击刘海直接打开截图页。

自动检查使用生产音乐解析器、控制器和图片加载方法，覆盖歌名／封面分开发送、迟到封面、连续切歌、无封面、共享专辑图、切换播放器和乱序下载，并完成 Apple Silicon 应用构建与签名复检。检查不控制用户正在运行的播放器或 Sapphire；播放器本身若延迟提供封面，期间仍会短暂显示占位图。
